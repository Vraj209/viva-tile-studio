FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What does VAVI Tile Studio do?
VAVI Tile Studio creates customized tile designs with a particular focus on 3D printed and dimensional tile concepts for residential, commercial, artistic and design applications.

Can I provide my own design?
Absolutely. You can begin with artwork, a photograph, pattern, logo, illustration, sketch or other visual reference.

What if I don’t have a design?
You don’t need one.

You can send us inspiration and explain what you’re imagining. We can discuss the direction and determine what is possible for your project.

Can you create a company logo on tile?
Logo and branded tile concepts are one of the applications we can explore for commercial projects. Send us your logo file, approximate dimensions and a photograph or description of the intended space.

Do you work with architects and interior designers?
Yes. We welcome collaborations with architects, interior designers, builders, developers and other creative professionals.

Can you create a large feature wall?
Potentially, yes. Large projects can be designed as coordinated tile installations depending on the design, tile specifications and intended application.

Send us the wall dimensions and your concept so we can review the project.

Can I order a single customized tile?
Availability will depend on the tile, dimensions, design and production requirements. Contact us with your idea and we can advise on the options available.

What tile sizes are available?
Tile sizes and substrates may vary depending on the design and project requirements. We’ll recommend appropriate options after reviewing your project.

Can the tiles be used outdoors?
Outdoor suitability depends on the tile substrate, printing method, finish, installation system and environmental exposure. Let us know that your project is intended for exterior use so the appropriate specifications can be reviewed.

Can the tiles be used in showers or other wet areas?
Suitability for wet environments depends on the selected tile, surface treatment, printing process and installation system. Please identify the intended application when requesting a quote.

How long does a custom project take?
Timelines vary depending on the project’s size, design complexity, approvals and production requirements. An estimated timeline can be provided once your project details have been reviewed.

How much does custom tile printing cost?
Every custom project is different.

Pricing can depend on factors including:

• Tile size
• Quantity
• Design complexity
• Printing requirements
• Surface/texture requirements
• Project dimensions
• Artwork preparation
• Finishing requirements

Submit your project details to receive a custom quotation.

Can I see the design before production?
For applicable custom projects, a digital design or project visualization can be prepared for review before final production approval.

Do you install the tiles?
Installation arrangements and requirements will depend on the project and location. Please mention if you require installation support when requesting your quote.
