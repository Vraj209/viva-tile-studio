## Prioritized manufacturer questions

**P0** means resolve before making the associated unqualified suitability, resistance, safety or certification claim. **P1** means resolve before approving the relevant product selection, quotation or installation specification. These priorities do not block publishing cautious, accurately attributed information. All groups remain **open**.

### 1 PEI and Mohs hardness

**Priority P0 — P18–P19.** Is a PEI classification available or applicable to these products? Was the answer under “PEI rating” intended only to state Mohs hardness? Which exact body, glaze, finish and product families achieve Mohs 5–6? What method was used, and does the value concern the glaze, relief face or another surface?

**Evidence requested:** Corrected PEI/Mohs fields, product scope and complete relevant hardness/abrasion reports with methods, dates, results and limitations. If PEI does not apply, request a clear manufacturer statement explaining that scope without suggesting a floor approval.

**Decision unlocked:** A correctly labelled, scoped hardness statement and an actual PEI classification only if applicable and substantiated. Do not publish “PEI 5–6.”

### 2 Exterior exposure and climate

**Priority P0 — P7 versus P11.** Are products limited to covered outdoor areas or also approved for fully exposed façades? How does suitability vary by body, glaze, finish, size, mounting system, climate, moisture saturation or temperature variation? What restrictions or additional review would apply to a freezing-climate application, including one in Canada?

**Evidence requested:** A written application matrix, exposure limitations, approved assemblies, climate/temperature limits, installation manual and product-specific warranty terms. Identify the supporting reports rather than repeating “all weather.”

**Decision unlocked:** Clear covered/exposed use guidance for named products and installation conditions. Until reviewed, retain project-specific confirmation and make no Canadian-winter or unrestricted climate claim.

### 3 Frost resistance and UV or color stability

**Priority P0 — P7 and P13.** Which tests support frost resistance and UV/color stability? Which products, bodies, glazes and metallic finishes were tested? What specimen preparation, exposure conditions, duration/cycles, pass criteria, measured change and limits apply?

**Evidence requested:** Full reports identifying the laboratory, method, tested products and dates, plus any restricted colors/finishes or exposure conditions. Ask whether accelerated results have a defined relationship to the proposed service conditions.

**Decision unlocked:** Precisely scoped resistance statements supported by the reviewed results. Absolute “100%,” “frost-proof,” “UV-proof” or permanent no-fade promises remain withheld unless independently supportable within a defined scope.

### 4 Fire classification and assembly scope

**Priority P0 — P34.** What classification document supports Class A1? Which standard and edition apply? Does it cover the tile alone or a complete installed assembly? Which bodies, finishes, coatings, thicknesses, adhesives, backing and mounting arrangements are included? What supports the separate zero-flame-spread/no-smoke wording?

**Evidence requested:** The complete classification and referenced test reports, issuer, report numbers, validity, scope and mounting/substrate conditions, including exclusions.

**Decision unlocked:** Accurate fire wording for the covered product or assembly. A tile classification must not be represented as approval of an entire façade system. No standard or equivalent classification should be substituted from general industry knowledge.

### 5 Lead and cadmium and broader safety claims

**Priority P0 — P32.** Does “free” mean absent from composition, not intentionally added, or below a tested release limit? Which methods, detection/reporting limits, thresholds and jurisdictional requirements apply? Do results cover every glaze/color, metallic finish and body? What separately supports “non-toxic,” “eco-friendly” and compliance with “safety norms”?

**Evidence requested:** Product-scoped composition/declarations and relevant test reports with numeric results, methods, thresholds and dates. Request the exact named norms and substantiation for any environmental claim.

**Decision unlocked:** A specific composition or release statement with defined scope and threshold, if supported. Do not equate no intentional addition with zero content, or a release test with unrestricted non-toxicity or environmental benefit.

### 6 Water absorption by body

**Priority P1 — P5.** Which SKUs/collections are the vitrified body below 0.5%, and which are the decorative ceramic body below 3%? How does the source's “porcelain” label map to those groups? Are the values thresholds, typical measurements or guaranteed limits, and are they valid across all sizes/finishes?

**Evidence requested:** A body-to-product schedule and complete absorption reports showing test method, sample scope, results and applicable tolerances or acceptance criteria.

**Decision unlocked:** Body-specific values tied to orderable products. Keep the two source values distinct; do not use either as an automatic approval for submerged, frost-exposed or other demanding applications.

### 7 Formats thickness and relief profile

**Priority P1 — P21 and P37–P44.** Are all seven metric formats available as single pieces across every body, collection and finish? Are those nominal or actual dimensions? Does 8–9 mm describe only the base body or the maximum total thickness including high relief? What are the dimensional, thickness, flatness, squareness and relief tolerances?

**Evidence requested:** An approved SKU/format matrix and dimensioned drawings or measured specifications, including maximum relief depth, tolerances, edge details and a corrected consistent metric/imperial schedule if imperial dimensions are required.

**Decision unlocked:** Accurate orderable format choices, total build-up/clearance information and product-specific quantity planning. Until confirmed, display source-listed metric options without implying universal availability or precise total relief thickness.

### 8 Weight packing and handling

**Priority P1 — P23.** Can weight per tile and per square metre be supplied for each format/body? What are pieces and net coverage per box, gross package/pallet weights, packing dimensions, breakage allowances if any, and required handling/lifting/storage procedures for the largest formats?

**Evidence requested:** Product-specific packing lists and weight schedules, handling guidance and any installation load data. Distinguish net tile mass from packaging and installed system mass.

**Decision unlocked:** Supported weight/packing information for quotations, transport, handling and installation review. Do not invent weights, box coverage, packaging counts or structural allowances.

### 9 Installation substrates joints and anchoring

**Priority P0 for exterior system claims and P1 for product selection — P26–P28.** Which substrates and preparation/moisture conditions are supported? What precise adhesive system, referenced standard/edition, coverage and application method are recommended? Do requirements change for large formats, heavy relief, wet exposure or exteriors? When is mechanical anchoring mandatory rather than recommended, and who designs/verifies the attachment system? What does “minimum 2–3 mm” mean in practice, and how are movement joints and edge alignment handled?

**Evidence requested:** A product-specific installation manual, compatible system/component schedule, substrate requirements and anchoring design responsibility/criteria, with relevant system reports. Request clear grout compatibility and joint-width instructions separately from movement-joint design.

**Decision unlocked:** A precise, qualified installation summary and a useful referral to project-specific system design. A C2TE reference or short anchoring recommendation alone cannot establish a complete approved high-rise façade solution.

### 10 Poolside exposure chemical resistance and maintenance

**Priority P0 for exposure claims and P1 for care instructions — P15, P17 and P30.** What moisture, repeated splash, water-feature, chlorine, salt and other chemical exposure is permitted for poolside backdrops? Does standing water or continuous wetting fall outside that scope? Which household cleaners, acids, detergent types, pH/concentrations, contact times and tools are allowed or excluded? Does the claimed non-porous glazed finish apply to faces, edges and all relief surfaces?

**Evidence requested:** A product/finish-specific care and chemical-compatibility guide, relevant chemical-resistance evidence, exposure exclusions and maintenance procedures. Request a written confirmation that the active-pool-tank restriction remains applicable.

**Decision unlocked:** Useful poolside and cleaning guidance without implying submerged suitability or universal cleaner resistance. Continue to show the active-pool-tank restriction and recommend only the source's basic soft-brush/microfiber/mild-detergent care while limits are resolved.

### 11 Manufacturing terminology and product identity

**Priority P0 — P3 versus earlier website brief.** How are these products made, and what does “handmade high-relief 3D” mean? Is the relief integral to the body, applied separately or created through another process? Does “custom 3D printed tiles” describe actual additive production, printing/decorating onto an existing tile, or a marketing description? Do both source documents concern the same manufacturer and product range?

**Evidence requested:** An approved process description, identified product samples, close photographs/sections showing the actual relief, and written authorization for the preferred public terminology. Request support for “high-strength” if that wording is to be retained.

**Decision unlocked:** A coherent manufacturing description and evidence-based product visuals. Preserve the older wording in its archive; do not silently reconcile it with the new datasheet or treat the generated flat-art concepts as photographs of the high-relief product.

### 12 Datasheet ownership origin and revision control

**Priority P1 — title and P36.** Who issued this datasheet, what is its current revision/date, and exactly which products does it cover? Is “Made in India” valid for every offered body/format/finish? Can the manufacturer supply a corrected, approved datasheet incorporating the resolved scopes and authorize its public distribution?

**Evidence requested:** Issuer/manufacturer identity, approved product scope, origin declaration/traceability as appropriate, revision-controlled datasheet and distribution authorization. Request named responsibility for future specification updates.

**Decision unlocked:** An attributable current source document and product-specific origin wording. Manufacturing origin does not define VAVI's launch territory, delivery coverage or installation network.
