## Critical factual boundary

The original business brief repeatedly uses **“custom 3D printed tiles”** and describes dimension and texture. That exact terminology is preserved in the source file. It is not sufficient evidence of additive manufacturing, a particular printer, raised physical relief, kiln firing or a specific coating. The interface may use the creative brief's concise proposed phrase “Custom tile designs created around your artwork, your ideas, and your space.” This does not silently revise the original source: manufacturing terminology remains an explicit owner confirmation before publication.

Treat a flat artwork image as artwork, a generated material study as a concept, and a verified photograph of a finished tile as product evidence. Never derive physical relief from painted shadows. No supplied source establishes an address, service area, currency, delivery times, contact email, telephone, installation network, pricing or legal destinations.

## Proposed visual and motion assumptions

The creative brief proposes an architectural editorial grid, large readable type, quieter navigation and forms, a single signature artwork, and restrained object presentation. In the absence of approved artwork/title samples, these are reversible art-direction choices rather than claimed reproductions. Any generated botanical or abstract study must be identified as a design exploration throughout the page.

A24 supplies an object-presentation reference, not proof that any particular disc, interaction or scene should be copied. IDST supplies a color-direction reference. Refer to the implementation's reference review for actually observed colors and behaviors. If exact style inspection is unavailable, the brief explicitly permits a provisional palette: Chalk `#F2EFE8`, Ink `#242822`, Forest `#303C32`, Stone `#D2C9BA`, Bronze `#92785E`. These values must never be called an IDST extraction without evidence. Bronze is decorative unless its actual text pairing passes contrast.

Recommended motion hierarchy: a brief hero settling transition; one shared progress model for the transformation; normal scroll navigation; static mobile and reduced-motion story; gallery and enquiry readable without animation. Any one artwork's reuse is narrative continuity, not evidence of several completed projects.

- [ ] **Verified surface relief:** Supply close product photographs or technical evidence and approve any relief/depth representation. Maintain a visually neutral surface until then.
